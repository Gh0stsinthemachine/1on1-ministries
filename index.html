Who We Are

Built on the belief that every child deserves to be seen.

1:1 Ministries is led by Diana, whose heart has always been with children facing hardship. We meet kids and families where they are — with resources, relationships, and real presence. No child should face uncertainty alone. Our work is personal, one-to-one, because that's how lives change.

"The name says everything. One ministry to one child. That's where transformation starts." — Diana
