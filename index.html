Who We Are

The system has an exit date. We don't.

1:1 Ministries was founded by Diana and her husband with one conviction: no young person should be handed a birthday and called independent without the tools, the guidance, or the roof to back it up. We exist to fill the gap the foster system leaves behind — with housing, mentorship, and the practical life skills nobody ever taught these kids.

"The system gave them a birthday and called it independence. We call it a starting line — and we'll be there at every step." — Diana

The Vision

25 tiny homes. One guidance center. A real foundation.

Diana and her husband are building something the foster system never offered: a physical place to land, surrounded by people who will walk with you as you learn to build your life.

25

Tiny Homes

Safe, dignified, independent housing for foster youth transitioning out of the system. A real address. A real home.

1

Guidance Center

An on-site hub for life skills workshops, one-on-one mentorship, job readiness, and community — everything a support system provides.

Why This Model Works

Housing alone doesn't build a life. Mentorship without stability doesn't stick. The 1:1 model combines both — giving young adults a safe place to live and the daily guidance to figure out what comes next. The goal isn't dependency. It's launching people into full, independent lives with the foundation they never got.

Full development plan coming soon. Diana and her husband are finalizing the details. Want to be part of the ground floor? Get in touch.

What We Teach

How to adult — for real.

Most people learned these things growing up, from family. Foster youth often didn't. Our guidance center covers the full picture of independent adult life.

💰

Money & Budgeting

Bank accounts, building credit, paying bills on time, avoiding predatory debt.

🏠

Housing

How to rent, understand a lease, pay utilities, and know your rights as a tenant.

💼

Employment

Résumés, interviews, workplace expectations — and keeping a job once you have it.

🏥

Healthcare

Insurance basics, making appointments, navigating prescriptions and mental health.

🍳

Food & Cooking

Grocery shopping on a budget, meal planning, and basic cooking that keeps you fed.

📋

Life Admin

Taxes, IDs, benefits enrollment, paperwork — all the bureaucracy of being an adult.

🤝

Relationships

Healthy communication, boundaries, conflict resolution, and building a real support network.

🙏

Faith & Purpose

Rooted in faith, open to all. We walk with young people as they discover who they are.
