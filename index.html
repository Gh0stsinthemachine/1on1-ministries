Who We Are

The system has an exit. We don't.

1:1 Ministries was founded by Diana with one conviction: no young person should have to figure out adulthood without someone in their corner. Foster youth aging out of the system face a cliff — no family safety net, no roadmap, no one to call when life gets complicated. We exist to be that someone. We mentor, we teach, we show up — one person to one person, for as long as it takes.

"The system gave them a birthday and called it independence. We call it a starting line — and we'll be there." — Diana

What We Teach

The things most of us learned at home.

Foster youth age out knowing how to survive the system. We teach them how to thrive outside it — the real-world skills that no one covered in school.

💰

Money & Budgeting

Opening a bank account, building credit, paying bills, avoiding debt traps.

🏠

Finding Housing

How to rent an apartment, understand a lease, and know your rights as a tenant.

💼

Getting a Job

Résumés, interviews, workplace expectations — and how to keep the job once you have it.

🏥

Healthcare Navigation

Insurance basics, how to make a doctor's appointment, and when to use the ER vs. urgent care.

🍳

Food & Cooking

Meal planning, grocery shopping on a budget, and basic cooking that doesn't require a culinary degree.

📋

Life Admin

Taxes, IDs, benefits, government paperwork — navigating the bureaucracy of being an adult.

🤝

Relationships & Conflict

Healthy communication, setting boundaries, and building a support network that lasts.

🙏

Faith & Purpose

Rooted in faith, open to all. We walk with young people as they discover who they are and what they're here for.

How to Help

You don't have to be a professional to change a life.

There's more than one way to show up for a young person who has never had someone in their corner. Find yours.

💛

Become a Mentor

Be the one consistent adult in someone's life. No special credentials required — just reliability, patience, and a willingness to show up.

🎓

Share Your Expertise

Are you in finance, HR, healthcare, or trades? Teach a workshop. Your everyday knowledge is transformative to someone starting from zero.

📦

Donate Goods

Household basics, work clothes, kitchen supplies, hygiene products. The things you take for granted are often the things they've never had.

💰

Give Financially

Every dollar funds mentorship, materials, and emergency support. Direct, transparent, and all going to the work.

📣

Spread the Word

Tell someone. Share this page. The biggest barrier to these young people getting help is that most people don't know this gap exists.

🙏

Pray With Us

We believe prayer is foundational. Lift up the young people navigating the hardest transition of their lives — and the community walking with them.
